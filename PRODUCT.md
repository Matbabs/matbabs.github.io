# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Outdoor sports enthusiasts - trail runners, climbers, cyclists, and mountain bikers who value community safety and shared vigilance. French market (🇫🇷).

## Product Purpose

A BLOC is a French outdoor sports app built on the principle of "esprit de cordée" (rope team spirit) - safety is collective, not individual. Each practitioner becomes a sentinel for others.

## Positioning

Real-time community safety alerts - the key differentiator is a live hazard reporting system where outdoor enthusiasts alert each other instantly, whether on bike, climbing wall, or trail.

## Operating Context

Users discover the app via web search, download on App Store or Google Play, then use the app for:

- Real-time hazard reporting to the community
- Live social map showing fellow ExploRiders and risk zones
- Customized training cycles for their goals and level
- AI assistant for training advice and adjustments

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play)
- French-language app and website
- Requires GPS location for activity tracking
- Email-based account system with optional profile photo

1. **Collective Safety** - No one rides alone; we watch out for each other
2. **Real-Time Community** - Live awareness of fellow ExploRiders and hazards
3. **Personalized Training** - Cycles tailored to individual goals and level
4. **AI-Powered Guidance** - Instant answers from the adventure AI assistant
